# Instant Pot Bean Stew

## Ingredients

- **12 oz** bacon, sausage, or ham
- **4 tbsp** minced garlic
- **1 lb** great northern beans (optionally soaked overnight)
- **1** green pepper
- **1** diced onion
- **1/2 lb** diced carrots
- **1 tsp** paprika
- **1** can of tomato paste
- **2 tbsp** worcestershire sauce
- **1/4 tsp** black pepper
- **1 tbsp** apple cider vinegar
- **1/2 cup** Chopped spinach or kale
- **3 1/2 cups** water

## Directions

Saute the meat in the instant pot. Set aside.

Add beans, vegetables, spices, vinegar, Worcestershire sauce, garlic and water to the instant pot.
Pressure cook on high for 50 minutes; let pressure release naturally for 10 minutes.

Add bacon, tomato paste and spinach/kale. Let it sit for a few minutes.
