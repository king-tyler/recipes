# Brownies

## Ingredients

- **3/4 cups** unsalted butter
- **3/4 cups** cocoa powder
- **1 tsp** vanilla
- **1 3/4 cups** brown sugar
- **3** eggs
- **3/4 cups** flour
- **1/2 tsp** salt
- **1/2 cup** chocolate chips

## Directions

Preheat the oven to 350°F. Greease a 9"x9" pan.

(Optional) Brown the butter in a pan.

Beat eggs and brown sugar together. Mix in butter and cocoa powder. Add flour and salt and mix.
Fold in chocolate chips.

Bake 20-30 minutes
