# Lentil Stew

## Ingredients

- **2 tbsp** olive oil
- **2** large onions, diced
- **2** medium carrots *or* **16** baby carrots, diced
- **2** large potatoes, diced
- **1 14 oz. can** diced tomatoes
- **2 cup** lentils, rinsed
- **1/2 lb** bacon, cooked and chopped
- **2 tsp** garlic powder
- **2 tsp** rosemary
- **2 tsp** thyme
- **2 tsp** paprika
- **2 tsp** chicken bouillon
- **5 1/2 cup** water

## Directions

Saute the carrots and onions. Add the remaining ingredients and simmer with a lid for 30 minutes.
